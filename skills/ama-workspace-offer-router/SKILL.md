# Workspace Offer Router

1. Read the destination repository README, AGENTS.md and claim governance. Respect its current stage.
2. Capture buyer, operator, participants, desired outcome, current breakdown, sensitivity, budget hypothesis and buying trigger. Mark missing answers; continue reversible analysis.
3. Route by central question: evidence sufficient to proceed → readiness review; agreed work being delivered → client delivery; independent parties aligning evidence → deal collaboration; founders learning contribution rules → founder lab. Combine modules only when the buyer needs both; preserve their distinct acceptance rules.
4. Specify a bounded offer: input limits, outputs, turnaround hypothesis, exclusions, acceptance criteria, operator, price experiment, variable cost, support allowance and stop rule. Never invent a validated price.
5. Separate evidence status (Verified, Demonstrated, Founder-Reported, Roadmap, Unknown/Requires Verification) from provenance and independent verification. A prototype is not commercial-readiness evidence.
6. Plan three to five pilots. Measure first useful result, setup effort, active contribution, support burden, willingness to pay and renewal intent. Record denominators and failures.
7. Require authorization, recovery/export/deletion, observability, operating ownership and billing checks appropriate to the release before recommending sale.
8. Output the fit decision, offer card, delivery handoff, commercial gates and one copy-paste task per agent. Keep client identities and files out of public strategy repos.

Use AMA Solutions for commercial ownership; use MAIM for education. Keep proposed names and prices explicitly experimental. Do not imply government, UN, UNESCO, Hanzo or Lux endorsement from brand presence.
