# AI & Cloud Cost Diagnostic — Expansion Path

How a Phase A diagnostic expands responsibly — without ever forcing the next sale. The north star holds: a diagnostic that recommends "do not proceed" is a success, not a failed expansion.

> Canonical spellings: Hanzo, Enso, Lux, AMA Solutions, Zach Kelling, MAIM.

## The ladder

1. **Phase A — AI & Cloud Cost Diagnostic** ($12–20K). Bounded, read-only, metadata-only. Ends with an honest go/no-go.
2. **Phase B — optional Enso Evaluation Pilot** ($8–15K). Only if Phase A justifies it **and** DR-6 is resolved. Measures cost/quality on a bounded workload. **⛔ BLOCKED pending DR-6 (owner: Zach Kelling).**
3. **Production Enso routing + platform usage** (elephant). Move live traffic to Enso routing; recurring platform fees by volume. Requires DR-6 resolved and a successful Phase B.
4. **Adjacent rabbits/elephants** as trust compounds: AI Governance & Data Exposure Audit, Private Knowledge Vault, Agentic Engineering Readiness Sprint (see commercialization `offer-portfolio.md`).
5. **Whale territory** (sovereign/regulated infrastructure) only for accounts whose potential warrants it, on the vertical's own timeline.

## Rules of expansion

- **Never manufacture the next step.** Expansion is offered only when the client's own data (Phase A) or results (Phase B) justify it. A "do not proceed" is logged as a clean, successful outcome — and often the seed of trust for a *different* rabbit later.
- **Track three things separately:** account potential (could be a whale), current offer size (a rabbit), and current stage (diagnostic). Do not conflate.
- **Gate the gated things.** Phase B and anything touching Enso data handling stay blocked until DR-6. Case-study naming stays gated until DR-7. Regulated-data pilots wait for the DR-6 policy to permit them.
- **Claim discipline travels up the ladder.** Larger engagements do not unlock larger claims; every step is still governed by `claim-governance.md`.

## Signals that justify expansion (not triggers to push)

- Phase A found **material, confirmed** waste or a defensible optimization hypothesis on a real workload.
- The client has budget/authority for production change and asked about next steps.
- The account has strategic or expansion value (which may also justify a strategic exception to the $20K floor for *related* offers — with AMA approval).

## What expansion is not
Expansion is not a quota on the diagnostic. If diagnostics stop recommending Phase B because clients are genuinely efficient, that is the system working honestly — and it is what earns AMA the trust that produces the larger engagements over time.
